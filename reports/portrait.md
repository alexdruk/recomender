# Portrait of my taste

Built 16 Nov 2026, 11:11 from what the reading model (qwen3.5:9b) says about each book. It reads 3 passages of about 2,500 words each, spread evenly over the first 25,000 words (about 100 pages) of the book. **294** books of my library against **427** ordinary new books picked at random from the daily updates. Each quality is rated from 1 to 5.

## In short

- My books stand out most by **more** plot originality (3.7 against 3.3), humour and irony (2.5 against 2.0), world-building (3.7 against 3.4), depth of characters (3.3 against 3.1).
- They have **less** weight of the romance line (1.6 against 2.2), emotional intensity (3.1 against 3.4), weight of explicit content (1.1 against 1.2).
- No real difference in richness of language, psychological complexity, literary polish, darkness and violence, plot pace.
- Narrative voice: tense tone in 22% of my books against 36%; ironic tone in 6% of my books against 2%; light tone in 21% of my books against 14%.
